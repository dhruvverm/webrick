The short version

Four things worth knowing.

  • We only collect what you choose to give us: your contact details and what your business needs.
  • We use it to reply to your enquiry, send a quote and do the work you ask for.
  • We never sell or rent your information.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete it, or tell us to stop contacting you, at any time.

This is a summary for convenience. The full policy below is what applies.

Webrick ("we", "us", "our") builds websites, online stores, web apps, mobile apps and custom software for businesses in India. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit www.webrick.in, contact us, or submit an enquiry through our advertisements on Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp, and how we use and protect it.

01

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, such as when you fill in an enquiry form or message us:

Contact details

Your name, phone number and email address.

Business details

Your business name, city, type of business, and what kind of website or software you need.

Your answers and messages

Answers to questions in our enquiry forms (for example, your budget or when you want to start) and any messages you send us on WhatsApp, phone or email.

Website usage

Basic technical information such as browser type, device and pages visited, collected through cookies or similar tools when you browse our website.

02

How we use your information

  • To contact you about your enquiry by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To prepare quotes and proposals and to provide the services you ask for.
  • To follow up on projects, invoices and support.
  • To improve our website, services and advertising.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

We will not send you marketing messages unrelated to your enquiry without your consent, and you can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

03

Enquiries from Facebook and Instagram ads

When you submit a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta Platforms shares the information you entered with us so we can respond to you. Meta's own handling of your data is covered by Meta's Privacy Policy.

04

Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with:

  • Service providers who help us run our business (for example hosting, email, messaging, CRM and payment providers), only as needed to provide their service to us.
  • Government or legal authorities when required by law.
05

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, and client project and billing records for as long as required by Indian tax and accounting laws. After that, we delete or anonymise it.

06

How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, such as access controls and secure, password-protected systems, to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

07

Your rights

In line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

Access

Ask what personal information we hold about you.

Correct

Ask us to correct or update it.

Delete

Ask us to delete it, unless we must keep it by law.

Withdraw consent

Withdraw your consent to being contacted at any time.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.

08

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies, including advertising and analytics tools such as the Meta Pixel, to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

09

Children

Our services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with its effective date.
