# Shop makeover · Ep. 01 — voice-over script

Record this in your own voice on your phone (Notes app → voice memo, or straight into the Instagram editor).
Talk like you'd explain it to a shop owner over chai: relaxed, a bit of a smile, no selling voice.
Each block lines up with a scene; the time is where that scene starts in the video.

**0:00 — Hook (empty Instagram profile on the phone)**
If I handled your furniture shop's marketing, here's what would happen from day one.

**0:04 — Google search**
First, people would actually find you. Someone searches "furniture shop near me" and there you are. Your hours, your number, and a four-point-eight.

**0:10 — Website**
They tap through to a website that opens in a second. Not a brochure. One job: a big button that says "message us on WhatsApp".

**0:18 — Instagram makeover**
Your Instagram gets a proper bio. What you make, where you are, when you're open. Highlights for sofas, beds, reviews. And a grid where every post has a reason, nothing random.

**0:28 — Ads**
Then the ads start. Not to the whole city. Only to people within ten kilometres who are looking for furniture this month.

**0:35 — WhatsApp**
Every enquiry lands on your WhatsApp, and on a list. So nobody gets forgotten, and nobody waits two days for a reply.

**0:43 — Result**
The result? Busier weekends. And you didn't post a single thing. You make the furniture. We bring the customers.

**0:49 — Call to action**
Want this for your shop? Send me the word "SHOP" on WhatsApp, the number's on screen, or tap the link in my bio. And tell me in the comments: which business should we make over next?

---

Recording tips
- One take per block is fine; pauses between blocks are natural and easy to cut.
- Say the numbers the way you'd say them out loud ("four-point-eight", "ten kilometres").
- If a line feels stiff, change it to how you'd actually say it. The captions on screen are the summary, not a transcript.
- Add the voice in the Instagram editor over the video, then a quiet background track at low volume.
