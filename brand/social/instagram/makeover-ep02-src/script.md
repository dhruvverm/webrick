# Brand makeover · Ep. 02 (clothing) — voice-over script

Record this in your own voice on your phone (Notes app → voice memo, or straight into the Instagram editor).
Talk like you'd explain it to a shop owner over chai: relaxed, a bit of a smile, no selling voice.
Each block lines up with a scene; the time is where that scene starts in the video.

**Hook (empty Instagram profile on the phone)**
If I handled your clothing brand's marketing, here's what would happen from day one.

**Photoshoot**
First, a proper photoshoot. Your pieces on real people, in good light, with a plan for which photo goes where. No more hanger shots on a bedsheet.

**Instagram makeover**
Your Instagram gets a bio that says what you sell, where you ship, and how to order. Highlights for the new drop, sizes and reviews. And a grid that looks like a brand, not a jumble.

**Online store**
Then a store that opens in a second. Collections, a size chart, pay by UPI or cash on delivery, and a WhatsApp button for the "is this available in medium" question.

**Ads**
Then the ads. Reels from the shoot, shown to women who've been browsing clothes this week, and again to the ones who looked at a dress and didn't buy.

**Orders and DMs**
Every DM gets answered, every order gets tracked, and your customers get a WhatsApp update when it ships. Nobody chases you asking where's my order.

**Result**
The result? Drops that sell out. And you didn't shoot, post or reply to a single thing. You make the clothes. We bring the customers.

**Call to action**
Want this for your brand? Send me the word BRAND on WhatsApp, the number's on screen, or tap the link in my bio. And tell me in the comments: which business should we make over next?

---

Recording tips
- One take per block is fine; pauses between blocks are natural and easy to cut.
- Say the numbers the way you'd say them out loud ("four-point-eight", "ten kilometres").
- If a line feels stiff, change it to how you'd actually say it. The captions on screen are the summary, not a transcript.
- Add the voice in the Instagram editor over the video, then a quiet background track at low volume.
